## 1.1 — THE WORLD (p.1–2)

### POST-APOCALYPTIC UNIVERSE (p.1)

The light of civilization has faded from this universe. The flames of wars, the collapse of governments, the disappearance of common knowledge. Megacities lie in ruin or abandoned. And humankind, reduced to small nomadic communities, wanders the endless void, seeking the scraps of fallen battles.

No one remembers how, when, or why it happened. But the age of humanity came to an abrupt and violent end several centuries ago.

The last remnants of our species live scattered across a hundred star systems, divided and without communication.

All knowledge of advanced technology was lost in the apocalypse. The survivors cobbled together what they could, rebuilt what had been salvaged from the rubble and ruins, repaired circuits, or watched in awe and fear those who could.

### UNTAMED SPACE (p.1)

This universe is a hostile environment for humanity. Some groups choose to live in orbit, aboard space stations, or in stations known as **Settlements**. But out in the void — in the vast untamed expanse of open space — nothing is guaranteed without a way of escape.

For the brave, there are other places of respite: space travel is possible but far from easy. The most reliable technology — **Starship** space travel — is limited but for those determined enough, journeys are possible. The untamed void between planets is filled with **space pirates**, **raiders**, and **bounty hunters**.

### THE OLD WORLD (p.1)

The time before the apocalypse is known as the **Old World**. Despite the centuries that have passed, remnants of old-world technology and information still remain — scattered throughout this world, waiting to be unearthed.

### RELICS OF AGES PAST (p.2)

The scarcity of advanced technology has turned ancient tech from the Old World into the most prized commodities for generations of hunters.

Les reliques les plus précieuses :
- **Starships**
- **Rifles / Military weapons**
- **Cybernetic implants**
- **Complex virtual hubs**

Driven by either conviction or necessity, those who choose to leave the safety of their communities venture into ancient ruins in search of these relics. These explorers are known as **Spacebornes**.

### BIRTH OF CONFLICT (p.2)

Centuries of scarcity and highly skewed resource distribution have isolated tech communities from each other. Each group labels the other as enemies in a zero-sum game. The Old Clans of these communities fight each other in an endless war of attrition — factions with opposing values and skewed views. This is known as **Mark Rivals**.

### THE FUTURE IS YOURS (p.2)

In **ASTROPRISMA**, you play the role of a **Spaceborne** starting their own journey across the known universe.

You will explore planets, ancient ruins, forsaken cities, and other locations, uncovering the secrets of the Old World.

Your choices will gradually shape the story and the universe — the places you go, your decisions on who to side with, and how the factions in the universe evolve. The universe will continue evolving as you continue exploring the galaxy.

## 1.2 — THE GAME (p.3–4)

### HOW TO PLAY (p.3)

ASTROPRISMA is a space exploration game. The goal is to **explore the entirety of the galaxy**, discovering new worlds and helping one of the **five factions** achieve one of their objectives.

### EXPLORATION CYCLES (p.3)

Gameplay is segmented into **exploration cycles**. Each cycle is the start of a new location. Each cycle, you :
1. **Move** on the map → costs **1 Fuel**
2. **Explore** your location → roll d6, resolve event, gain resources, update Character Sheet & Journal

### CYCLE STEPS (p.3)

**1. MOVE YOUR STARSHIP**
Move your ship **1 Hex** on the Star System's Hex Map. Costs **1 Fuel**.

**2. EXPLORE LOCATION**
Roll a **d6** for the Exploration Roll and refer to the Location table. Encounter and journal the result.

**3. MARK DETAILS**
Draw your findings on the **map** and write about the encounter in your **Journal**.

### MODES OF PLAY (p.3)

| Mode | Description | Durée |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick Mode** | Résolution rapide, notes courtes, événements résumés | 1–2h |
| **Journaling Mode** *(Recommandé)* | Narration détaillée, descriptions de scènes, immersion totale | 2h+ par cycle |

### DICE NOTATION (p.3)

| Notation | Signification |
|---|---|
| **d6** | Roll a single six-sided die |
| **2d6** | Roll 2 six-sided dice, add results (2–12) |
| **d66** | Roll 2 six-sided dice, read separately as two-digit number (11–66) |
| **d6+1** | Roll a six-sided die, add 1 to the result |
| **d6×[stat]** | Roll a six-sided die, multiply result by a stat value |

### SETTING UP THE GAME (p.4)

Pour jouer, il faut :
- Un set de **Character Sheets**
- Un **token** pour marquer la position du personnage sur la carte
- Un **crayon** *(pas un stylo)* — pour noter les événements de l'aventure
- Un set de **dés polyédriques** ou un lanceur numérique :

| Lettre | Dé |
|---|---|
| A | 1 four-sided die (d4) |
| B | 1 six-sided die (d6) × 2 |
| C | 1 six-sided die (d6) |
| D | 1 eight-sided die (d8) |
| E | 1 ten-sided die (d10) |
| F | 1 twelve-sided die (d12) |
| G | 1 twenty-sided die (d20) |

*Les dés servent à déterminer : Health, Energy, Hull, Fuel.*

### STARTING YOUR ADVENTURE (p.4)

- Créer son **Player Character** (→ p.9) et choisir son **Origin** (→ p.11)
- Placer son token sur la **Starting Location** au centre de la carte
- Explorer une tuile dans n'importe quelle direction pour commencer le **premier cycle**

## 1.3 — EXPLORATION (p.5–6)

### STAR SYSTEM MAP (p.5)

Explorer la **Star System Map** est l'objectif principal du jeu. La carte est hexagonale et divisée en **rings** (→ p.63).

### EXPLORATION ROLLS (p.5)

À chaque cycle d'exploration :
1. Déterminer dans quel **ring** on se trouve
2. Roll d6 → **ENCOUNTER** : identifier la rencontre
3. Roll d6 → **ADDITIONAL** : informations supplémentaires / modifier la rencontre

### PLANETS & SATELLITES (p.5)

Quand un nouveau **hex planète** est découvert :
- Roll d6 sur la **Planets table** (→ p.73)
- Roll d6 sur la **Satellites table** (→ p.81)

Si la planète a des satellites → explorer les satellites lors des visites suivantes du hex.

**EXPLORE** : Découverte d'une petite planète → roll sur la table d'environnement planétaire. À la 3ème visite, l'environnement devient permanent.

### OUTER RING (p.6) → détails p.63

- Zone la plus **grande** et la plus **volatile** du système
- **Asterion Fields** communs → rendent la navigation difficile
- Repaire des **space pirates** et **outlaws** pour le commerce illégal et la contrebande

### MIDDLE RING (p.6) → détails p.65

- Située entre l'Outer Ring et l'Inner Ring
- Températures confortables (**110–180 jours** de climat tempéré)
- Dans l'Old World : settlements humains et société développée ici
- Lieu de découverte de **Star Volumes** (grands clusters) et de **dépôts de ressources inhabituels**

### INNER RING (p.6) → détails p.67

- Borde l'étoile centrale du système
- Températures **extrêmement élevées**
- **Solar Flares** intenses à intervalles irréguliers → dangereux pour la navigation
- **Helio Forms** (barrières énergétiques) → bloquent fréquemment la navigation des vaisseaux

## 1.4 — ORACLE (p.7–8)

### Qu'est-ce que l'Oracle ? (p.7)

The **Oracle System** is an artificial intelligence software from the Old World that has been developed and pre-configured to aid questioners in their exploration missions.

Rôle : aider les joueurs à répondre à toute question **Yes/No** ou **Open-Ended** pendant le jeu.

### YES/NO QUESTIONS — Table d6 (p.7)

| Roll | Réponse | Signification |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No, And** | Negative + complication |
| 2 | **No** | Negative answer |
| 3 | **No, But** | Negative + bonus |
| 4 | **Yes, But** | Positive + complication |
| 5 | **Yes** | Positive answer |
| 6 | **Yes, And** | Positive + bonus |

### TABLES ORACLE — Mots-clés (p.7–8)

**Groupe A (d6) :**
Submission · Regression · Collision · Control · Electronics · Occult

**Groupe B (d6) :**
Survival · Resilience · Conflict · Contact · Subterfuge · Emergence

**YES, BUT — mots-clés (p.8) :**
Nurturing · Light · Noise · Unifying · Malaise · Finesse

**YES — mots-clés (p.8) :**
Compromise · Propriety · Evolution · Guidance · Growth · Nature

**YES, AND — mots-clés (p.8) :**
Balance · Wealth · Change · Freedom · Harmony · Truth

### OPEN-ENDED QUESTIONS (p.8)

Open-Ended Questions permettent une interaction plus élaborée avec l'Oracle. Elles sont abstraites et servent de **narrative prompt** pour construire l'histoire.

Si la réponse de l'Oracle ne fait pas sens dans le contexte → recommencer le tirage.

**Exemple de jeu :**
> Tu rencontres un objet étrange dans une station déserte. Tu demandes à l'Oracle s'il semble d'origine humaine → réponse négative. Tu poses ensuite une Open-Ended Question sur son origine. Tu combines le résultat avec ton environnement pour construire une description vivante de sa nature.
