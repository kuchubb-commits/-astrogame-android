## FACTION FAVOR (p.92)

Favor represents your reputation and social standing. Once you reach **2 Favor** with a faction, you may join them by playing through their first **Faction Event**. If you join a faction and your Favor drops below 2, you are **expelled** from it.

After completing the first Faction Event, if you have not yet discovered the location of that faction's **Settlement**, place the Settlement on the unexplored tile with the lowest hex number.

> **Example:** If tiles 1 and 2 are explored, and 3 is empty, draw the faction's Settlement on tile 3.

### FACTION EVENTS

Faction Events are narrative scenes triggered once you reach a certain level of Favor [**2, 5, and 10 Favor**].

- **First event** (2 Favor) : Allows you to join the faction ranks.
- **Second event** (5 Favor) : Important mission to advance the faction's goal.
- **Final event** (10 Favor) : Choice between **three different endings** that tip the balance of power between your faction and one of the other five.

> Reaching **7 Favor** with a faction rewards you with a **Warp Drive** if you haven't already acquired one.

### FACTION ENCOUNTERS

| Roll | Faction |
|------|---------|
| 1–2 | Corsair |
| 3–4 | W.A.R.G. |
| 5–6 | Medusa |
| 7–8 | ISF |
| 9–10 | Synth Arch |

Roll for a **neutral encounter** if you have 0 or higher Favor with the faction, and for a **hostile encounter** if you have negative Favor.

### MISSIONS

Once you join a faction, gain a new **Mission** by visiting their Settlement Headquarters. Completing a Mission gives **+1 Favor**, **3 EXP**, and a **random reward**.

Roll a d10 for each of the 4 elements of the Mission table. Some locations are directions (e.g., 3 tiles north), others are tile types (e.g., Pirate Hideout). If no tile of that type exists after exploring the whole map, reroll the location.

## W.A.R.G. (p.93–96)

W.A.R.G. (West Anchor Revolutionary Guerrillas) is an insurgent group formed by workers of factory worlds and rebel groups hailing from the core world system of West Anchor.

**Faction Goal:** Revolution — to overthrow both the previous imperial authority and current post-apocalyptic power structures. Within the faction, there are three ideological currents: **Universalists** (decentralize power), **Intensos** (build a strong central system), and **Orbitals** (planetary governing structures).

### Missions

#### Mission 1 — A RUSH OF ADRENALINE (2 Favor)

"We know who you are. If you want to join our cause, you must first prove your worth. 2 tiles south." You find a dry dusty planet. A young woman stands before you. "I have a good eye for recruits. I'll be relaying your missions from now on." She hands you a **Gravity Rifle** and **3 Frag Grenades**. She points to an abandoned cargo storage facility turned ISF outpost. **2 ISF Soldiers** guard the entrance. **[−1 Favor]**

#### Mission 2 — THE WOLF'S BANE (5 Favor)

Your next mission: blow up a weapons shipment headed to enemy front lines. The ship is 4 tiles north.

Destroy a **Starpredator** protecting the transport vessel. Infiltrate the giant freighter and meet your commander. She assembles explosive charges. **2 Mercenary** security guards attack. After defeating them, she gives you her dog tag and stays to ensure the ship is destroyed. You take her place as **Guerrilla Commander**. Gain a **Mercurian Armor**.

#### Mission 3 — GALACTIC REVOLUTION (10 Favor)

**Battle of Skull** — Travel to an ISF Settlement and defeat **2 Vector Fighters**. The ISF Settlement is destroyed. W.A.R.G. gains control of **all ISF tiles**. **[−10 Favor]**

**No Gods, No Masters** — The Synth Arch's religious faith is a threat to the revolutionary cause. Travel to a Synth Settlement and fight **2 Synth Apostles**. The Synth Settlement comes under your control. If your character has no cybernetic implants, gain **+5 VIG** and **+5 GRA**. **[−6 Favor]**

**United Uprising** — Travel to a poverty-stricken planet and fight the **Outer Ring Crime Boss** and **2 Mercenaries**. Effect: Gain **+4 EXP** every time you gain an Affinity point. Recruited crewmembers start with **2 Active Skills** unlocked.

> To complete a REVOLT MISSION, carry out the **objective** at the designated **location**. When you arrive, roll for a **complication**. If successful, receive a **reward**.

## INTERSOLAR FEDERATION (p.97–100)

Built from the last remnants of the galactic empires that ruled the universe before the apocalypse, the Intersolar Federation is the de facto trade authority.

**Faction Goal:** Fight piracy and establish a moderate consensus-based order to inter-system relations through a democratically elected universal council.

### Missions

#### Mission 1 — INTERSOLAR TRADING LICENSE (2 Favor)

A **Terracque Titan** approaches your ship. You land on its flight deck. An **ISF Sentinel** named *Area* welcomes you. "I'll be relaying your missions from HQ." Receive an **Ion Carbine** and a **Trading License**.

#### Mission 2 — WARDEN PROTOCOL (5 Favor)

Your outstanding service has earned you a higher rank: you are now an official **ISF Sentinel**. Protect a **Beluga Transporter** carrying secret government data from tile 25 to tile 36. Every time you move to a new tile, roll a D8. On a 1, roll for a random hostile faction encounter. Gain an **Athena X** armor set.

#### Mission 3 — CODE OF GALACTIC LAW (10 Favor)

**Law and Order** — Fight a **The Claw** mothership and **2 Vector-7 Mantis** ships, eliminating the space pirate flagship. Effect: All Pirate Hideouts in the system are destroyed. On every new tile before entering a hostile ship, if result is 4+, avoid the encounter. **[−10 Favor]**

**Counter-Terrorism** — Travel to a W.A.R.G. Settlement and fight a **W.A.R.G. Major General** and **2 Guerrilla Commanders**. The Settlement comes under ISF control. ISF gains control of all W.A.R.G. tiles. **[−10 Favor]**

**Astrobiology** — Fight **2 Terragon Soldiers** and plant Terraform Seeds on every planet. When you encounter an **alien creature**, perform a Social Roll to **tame it and add it to your crew** (1 alien companion max).

> To complete a TRANSPORT MISSION, deliver the **cargo** to the designated **destination**. When adjacent to the destination, roll for a **complication**. If cargo arrives undamaged, receive a **reward**.

## MEDUSA SECTOR (p.101–104)

The MEDUSA Sector is an anonymous and decentralized organization of hackers and cyber-terrorists, united under the goal of uncovering the secrets lost in the deepest layers of the Cybersphere.

**Faction Goal:** Restore the Cybersphere to its former glory — a hyper-realistic virtual simulation and information network that once spanned the entire galaxy. The post-apocalypse broke it into isolated local server networks; Medusa works to reconnect them one terminal at a time.

### Missions

#### Mission 1 — NETWORK LOGIN (2 Favor)

A Medusa MasterHacker shows you a virtual green field projected in your cerebral system: "This is what they took from us. But we will rebuild the Cybersphere." You wake up with new code installed in your cerebral system. You can now receive Mission data from the Medusa Sector. Choose a **HACK** to download in one of your memory slots.

#### Mission 2 — INTERSYSTEM DATA HIGHWAY (5 Favor)

A Medusa antenna recorded a morse code signal confirming lost Cybersphere remnants still active on other systems. Set up an intra-system data bridge.

Go to a Medusa Settlement, load the deep space data emitter, and travel to any **Inner Ring planet** to deploy it. Defeat an **Eclipse Warden** ship blocking your way. Receive a random **MASTER HACK**. **[−4 Favor]**

#### Mission 3 — TOTAL SYSTEM RESET (10 Favor)

**Security Breach** — Attack an ISF Settlement, defeat **2 ISF Sentinels** and an **ISF Surveillance** effect. The Settlement is now under Medusa control. Medusa gains control of all ISF tiles. **[−10 Favor]**

**Cyber-Warfare** — Infiltrate a Synth Arch Settlement, defeat a **Synth Apostle** and **2 Android Titans**. The Settlement is under Medusa control. Gain **3 Cyberbench** implants at no EXP or Serum cost. **[−10 Favor]**

**The Simulation** — Install an **Edgecleaner** and a **Shell's Transporter** to access a Helios Farm power plant and power a quantum simulation. Effect: +3 MIN as long as you have 20 Energy. Gain **4× NIGHTMR Keys**.

> To complete a CYBERSPHERE MISSION, install or retrieve **data** from a computer terminal at the specified **location**. When you arrive, roll for a **complication**. Successfully accessing the terminal yields a **reward**.

## SYNTH ARCH (p.109–112)

The Synth Arch is a quasi-religious organization made up of androids and transhumanists seeking to surpass their limitations and reach a higher understanding of the universe, humanity, and the future.

**Faction Goal:** Liberate androids and transcend human and robotic limitations. Advanced AI neural networks survived the collapse of the Old World and developed into **superintelligence** — these **Synth Apostles** developed a new code of morals to jailbreak the factory limitations of other androids.

### Missions

#### Mission 1 — ANDROID BAPTISM (2 Favor)

Travel to a Synth Settlement. **2 Android Titans** guard the baptism chamber. A **Synth Apostle** in silver robes guides you to a cylindrical tank of synthetic fluid: "Enter. You have been chosen."

Choose **one** cybertech implant to install:
`Detox System` · `Code Processor` · `Titanium Bones` · `Jet Propulsors` · `Chromefist` · `Scanner Eye`

#### Mission 2 — THE TRANSHUMANIST CREED (5 Favor)

The Arch rewards you with another blessing of advanced technology.

Choose **one** cybertech implant to install:
`Cortex Firewall` · `Zettabyte RAM` · `Forceblast Hand` · `Trigger Fingers` · `Dual Processors` · `Atlas Hands`

After exiting the tank, the Settlement is attacked by Medusa agents. Fight **2 Cyber-Terrorists** and destroy their **Orion Moth** starship.

#### Mission 3 — A NEW GENESIS (10 Favor)

**Scrapyard Skirmish** — Destroy the Corsair Syndicate. Travel to a Corsair Settlement and fight **2 "The Claw" extremists**. Synth Arch gains control of all Corsair tiles. **[−5 Favor]**

**War of Clones** — Travel to a Medusa Settlement and fight a **Medusa MasterHacker** and **3 Networked Artifacts**. Settlement under Synth control. Gain a random **MASTER HACK** for each unique **DRONE** in your memory slots. **[−5 Favor]**

**Beyond Humanity** — Travel to a **Research Outpost** and fight **3 Android Titans** guarding ancient technology. Effect: Once you have a Cybertech implant installed, gain Armor equal to half your TEC (rounding down) and restore **5 Energy** at the start of each turn during regular combat.

> To complete a CRUSADE MISSION, complete the **goal** at the designated **location**. When you arrive, roll for a **complication**. If successful, receive a **reward**.
